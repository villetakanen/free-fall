# FREE//FALL rules prose brief

Editorial brief for The Other Side, grounded in [DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md#copy-register), the project's web-native publishing intent, and the user's request for brief, lightweight rules.

- **Clear first, themed second.** Use second person, present tense and active verbs. The voice is confident and clipped; dark humor is optional, filler is not.
- **Put the procedure first.** Tell the GM what to record, spend or roll. Cut scene-setting that delays the rule.
- **Make attitude concrete.** Costs, equipment and choices carry the cyberpunk tone. Avoid theatrical opening triplets, generic danger, slogans and forced jokes.
- **Use familiar words.** Keep necessary rules terms consistent. Avoid “NPC,” design commentary and explanations of why the subsystem exists.
- **Write each rule once.** Link to established Harm, Binding and machine rules. Keep only the differences needed here.
- **State the action directly.** Replace repeated “not,” “does not automatically,” and hypothetical misuse warnings with a positive rule or a link.
- **Keep the page small.** Short paragraphs, few headings, one useful example. Tables and lists must make an actual comparison or procedure easier to use.

For this pass: aim for 250–350 words. Preserve shared Reserve, ordinary versus main adversary declaration order, normal action/Harm rules, equipment release and separate machine resources. The edit changes presentation, not mechanics.
