# The Fixer

| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stitch | Luna (ex-NCI) | 57 | F |

A plan survives to the first hatch. After that, a crew is exactly as good as what it can repair — gear, ship, people, in roughly that order of difficulty. The Fixer is the one who repairs all three, and the reason experienced crews pay for one is that they've all lived the job where nobody did.

Stitch was NCI once: a researcher, good enough that Earth still has a warrant out for what she took with her when she left — espionage and sabotage, the paperwork says, and the paperwork is not wrong. She came out the far side of that with two convictions that never leave her: that every flag and every logo is the same animal wearing a different collar, and that the only thing worth trusting is a tool simple enough to understand completely. She fixes people for a living now. It's the one repair nobody's found a way to invoice a lie into.

She keeps no intrusive hardware in her own body — a hard line, given where she used to work. What she does keep is the suit.

## Starting position

At the [crew briefing](/scenarios/northern-lights/briefing/), choose where you first deploy. Keep the Havoc in either case:

- **With the crew vessel initially:** respond to damage or casualties there, then cross under your own thrust when needed. The first boarders do not have your hands beside them immediately.
- **Alongside the first boarders:** cross independently with the insertion and provide medical support at the chosen entry. The crew vessel must manage without your immediate presence.

The Havoc does not fit the approach shuttle. Its EVA thrusters, sealing, and twelve-hour life support let you cross without one, either at the start or later. Travel takes time, defenses can contest the route, and the frame needs an opening it can pass through. For a ram, either ride inside crash protection or establish your separate crossing; the collision does not move you from one group to the other.

## In this scenario

- Your Havoc looks ready for a heavier fight than you’ve equipped it for. Most of its mounts are empty. At a distance, that may not be obvious.

## Hooks (draft)

- The oldest hand on the boat, and the only one whose body has no firmware. She distrusts anything she can’t fix by hand.
- Left NCI over something she still won't name, and took the means to hurt them with her. Hasn't used it. Hasn't thrown it away either.
