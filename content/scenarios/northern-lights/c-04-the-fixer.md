# The Fixer

| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stitch | Earth | 47 | M |

Keeps people breathing and gear running with duct tape and bypasses — the luxury item this crew can't usually afford. At three players harm sticks and nobody is coming; that's the genre. When the fourth player sits down, the table gets a medic, and the preface's "statistically, at least one Harm Slot" stops being a death sentence.

**Optional — seat 4.**

## In this scenario

- Vacuum medicine: every wound in this scenario happens *in a suit*. Patching a person means patching the seal first. Make that vivid once and the table checks seals forever.
- **Skill variant worth offering the player: Clocksmith (AI).** In a scenario that is wall-to-wall thinking machines, the Fixer is the one character *qualified to talk to what's aboard* — trade the medic reserve for the most dangerous conversation in the box.

## Hooks (draft)

- Patched people on both sides of a corporate war and invoiced both. Considers this professional ethics, rigorously applied.
- Carries a coil of actual, physical duct tape. It has never once failed them. (Foreshadowing: analog survives what wireless doesn't.)
