# The Wirehead

| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Root | Al Presa (Free City) | 26 | X |

Guns get a crew aboard. They don't get it paid. At the end of every contract worth taking there is a system — a vault, a ledger, an array — and the payday goes through it. The Wirehead is the one who talks to it, and everyone else on the boarding roster is, on some honest level, an escort.

Root has been talking to machines since before it was legal for them to answer. Al Presa raised them — the free city, where a criminal record in every proper nation is a judicial opinion, not a fact — and the body shops finished what the arcologies started. The X on the manifest is Root's own filing: this much machine doesn't owe anyone a category.

The rest of the crew wears its survival — suits, frames, seals. Root grew theirs. No exo, no EVA shell: an oxygenator where the lungs used to negotiate and a pressure-woven dermis where the skin used to be, and if the hull opens, Root can keep working, in their own skin, on borrowed minutes. They hold a working theory that there is no such thing as a system without a back door.

- **Initial Attributes:** Ghost 12, Mind 8, Body 6
- **Action Pool:** 5d20
- **Harm slots:** 3, initially empty
- **Gear Loadout:**
  - Combat Deck: tactical analysis unit — the cracking suite. *[Binds 3 Mind]*
  - Ghost Link: operator's slate. *[Binds 2 Ghost]*
  - [The Wrench](/gear/utility/): EMR remote control module, operated through the Neural Link. *[Binds 1 Mind before Load Bearing]*
- **Augmentations:**
  - Neural Link: data port behind the ear. *[Binds 3 Ghost]*
  - Internal Oxygenator: artificial lung. *[Binds 3 Body]*
  - Dermal Vacuum Weave: pressure-sealed skin graft. *[Binds 2 Body]*
- **Calculations:**
  - Mind Binding: 3 (deck) + 1 (Wrench) = 4; both systems run through the Neural Link, whose Mind 8 Load Bearing covers all 4. Net Mind Binding: **0**.
  - Ghost Binding: 2 (slate) + 3 (link) = 5 · Body Binding: 3 (oxygenator) + 2 (weave) = 5
  - The Neural Link has 4 Mind of its allowance left for additional software or systems accessed through it. The Ghost Link separately covers up to 6 Mind of software run on the slate; never offset the same cost twice. Active programs beyond applicable allowances reduce Current Mind capacity.
- **Current Attributes:**
  - **Body:** 6 − 5 = **1** (one point that is still meat — the machinery is the body now)
  - **Mind:** 8 − 0 = **8** (before additional active software exceeds the applicable Load Bearing allowances)
  - **Ghost:** 12 − 5 = **7** (the deep sense of self that lets a person carry this much machine and stay a person)
- The pools *are* the gear: while everything runs, Root is cracker, ghost, and vacuum-walker at once. Break the deck, vent the slate, tear the weave — each loss doesn't just hurt, it deletes a capability. Everyone on this crew is a wizard until their wand snaps.

## Starting position

At the [crew briefing](/scenarios/northern-lights/briefing/), choose where you first deploy:

- **Inside the crew vessel:** provide systems support from shelter through remote interfaces the crew can actually reach. A sensor contact alone does not give you access to an isolated device.
- **With the boarding team at its chosen entry:** reach local controls and disconnected electronics directly. The Wrench works within 5 cm of a casing, subject to shielding; that puts your remaining Body reserve close to the danger.

Your vacuum-survival augmentations give you minutes of working exposure, not a means of propulsion. Arrange the crossing, return route, and a place to work before going outside. You can join later if shelter was the better starting position, or withdraw if physical access becomes untenable.

## In this scenario

- No network? Get closer. The Wrench can reach electronics within 5 cm when shielding permits. Find the device, establish access, and work from there.
- Your weave and oxygenator let you work in vacuum without a suit. Watch your exposure time; you still need a way back inside.
