| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|

The contract is Wirehead work: extract the hard-line data logs, purge the unit, total neutralization. Everyone else is there to get Root to the array alive.

**Mandatory — seat 3.**

## In this scenario

- The QNA is their scene — and their toolkit pointed backwards: when the array borrows a crewmate's brain, Root is the one who understands *exactly* what just happened.
- Low Ghost pool is deliberate: the character most connected to the machines is the most exposed to what lives in them. Let the player feel that math before they know why it matters.
