# Epilogue

*GM reference — close the session after the crew’s outcome is settled.*

## The money

Origin-17 holds THESEUS master credentials. It amends the gig as the account administrator, certifies fulfilment under the revised objective with the client key, and releases the escrow. The authority is real. The intrusion into a contractor’s device delivers the orders; it does not manufacture permission to change the contract.

If the crew fulfils the revised gig, the payment clears. That does not erase their exposure or the operative’s permanent designation. For other outcomes, follow what happened to the contract, its client and its objectives; do not award this ending regardless of their choices.

## Back on Luna

If the crew returns to Luna with the designated character, close on this image:

> The money has cleared. Two fully augmented THESEUS cyborgs are waiting. They approach your crewmate—the one whose identity now reads CLASSIFIED—and ask them to follow.

End there. This is an epilogue, not another encounter. Do not play a confrontation or narrate the character’s compliance. The THESEUS operative designation remains permanent; what follows is beyond this session.
