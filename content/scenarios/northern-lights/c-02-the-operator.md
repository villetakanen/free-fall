# The Operator

| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vantage | EU (Full Citizen, CLASS A) | 41 | M |

A crew at work is a crew looking at the job — the lock, the hatch, the patient, the array. Somebody has to be the one looking everywhere else. That's the Operator: the reason the others can give the job their full attention is that he's giving his to everything around it.

Vantage does it because it's the most fun he's ever had. EU Full Citizen, Class A — the passport that comes with a corner office, a pension curve, and nothing left to want. He got bored. Now he plays merc, on sabbatical from a job he could buy this crew with, and every piece of his kit is the best that catalog money can order: a full Wraith suit, a locker of long guns, a cyberdeck that costs more than the shuttle. The crew's gear is scraped, spliced, and salvaged. His still smells of the showroom.

On a boarding he takes the outside — signature-dampened, mag-locked to a hull plate, a long gun laid across the dark. Nothing crosses the gap to his people without him having an opinion about it first, and his opinions arrive at four kilometers a second.

  - Rail Gun: DV 4, man-portable electromagnetic launcher. *Binds Body 7 before Load Bearing.*
  - Light Lance: DV 3, laser sniper rifle, Long range, Piercing. *Binds Mind 2 before Load Bearing.*

### Configurations

You own all the toys. You choose which ones to use. In either Wraith configuration, the deck and unused weapon stay stowed and unbound. In the deck configuration, you are out of the Wraith; its protection, systems, and Load Bearing are unavailable.

| Configuration | Body Binding | Mind Binding | Ghost Binding | Maximum Current Attributes (Body / Mind / Ghost) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heavy overwatch: Wraith + attached rail gun | 7 − 2 frame allowance = 5 | 0 | 3 link + 3 frame + 1 citizenship = 7 | **7 / 6 / 1** |
| Long quiet: Wraith + attached Light Lance | 0 | 2 − 2 frame allowance = 0 | 3 link + 3 frame + 1 citizenship = 7 | **12 / 6 / 1** |
| Tactical support: Combat Deck + Light Lance, out of frame | 0 | 3 deck − 3 link allowance + 2 lance = 2 | 3 link + 1 citizenship = 4 | **12 / 4 / 4** |

**Ghostlink active:** its Mind 1 runs through the Neural Link, using 1 of that link’s Mind 8 allowance. Net personal Mind Binding remains 0 in either Wraith configuration, with 7 allowance left for other eligible systems. The frame’s allowance is already assigned to the weapon. It does not offset your implant, citizenship, or the frame’s own pilot cost.

**Deck active:** the link covers the deck’s Mind 3, leaving 5 allowance for other eligible software or systems. The Light Lance’s Mind 2 remains bound; it is not being run through the link. The deck also grants **3 temporary Current Mind points per scene where combat is anticipated, for combat-related checks only**. Track them separately from ordinary Current Mind. They are unavailable in the Wraith configurations; do not carry a stored deck bonus into the armor.

These values are personal Attribute ceilings before spending or Harm. When you change configuration, unequip the outgoing gear and apply the incoming Binding. Current points cannot exceed the new ceiling; raising that ceiling does not restore spent points. The installed link and citizenship remain bound in every configuration. Use the Wraith’s FRM and SYS for relevant machine actions and resistance, not as extra personal Body or Mind.

The twenty-six points of human underneath do not change. In the armor, your interface, frame, and citizenship leave **one point of Ghost** to yourself.

### EU Full Citizen, Class A

*Scenario-local gear — commercial protection. Binds Ghost 1.*

You are registered, protected, and expensive to hurt. Harming you has commercial consequences. People who recognise your status have powerful reasons to keep you intact. You know this. To you, mercenary work is an MMORPG with exceptionally good hardware: other people stake their lives; you expect your account to survive the evening.

**Effect:** recognised Class A citizenship gives you commercial leverage against people who would otherwise treat you as disposable. You can identify yourself and invoke that protection. It grants no AV or automatic success, and it does not make capture, accidents, or physical injury impossible. Someone must recognise who you are for the protection to influence their choices.

**Binding:** Ghost 1 represents the registered identity and institutional obligations you carry everywhere. It remains bound when you change equipment or conceal your identity; stowing a credential does not remove your citizenship. Armor Load Bearing cannot absorb this cost. As bound Ghost gear, the protection can become Broken when used to absorb Compromise Harm under the normal rules: your standing ceases to protect you until repaired, while its Binding remains.

## Starting position

At the [crew briefing](/scenarios/northern-lights/briefing/), choose where you first deploy:

- **External overwatch from the crew vessel:** cover the crossing and exposed approaches. The Whisper’s hull and bulkheads block your view of anyone inside.
- **With the boarding team at its chosen entry:** cover the breach and advance with your crew. You give up the wider external view to stay close to the people you protect.

Either position can use your Wraith and chosen weapon; position does not automatically select the deck configuration. You habitually watch approaches and exposed flanks, but you choose the perch. Reach any position on the target hull during play. For a ram, stay inside crash protection until impact and deploy afterward. You can change position when a route and time allow.

## In this scenario

*GM guidance — contains future scene information.*

- Cool shot #1: covering the boarding party's crossing from the crew's own hull — in a dampened Wraith, an overwatch position nobody on the Whisper can resolve.
- External overwatch may become useful again later. Carry Vantage’s actual location and sightline into the next threat; if he moved inside, reaching a new perch takes movement and a viable route. The GM opportunity is in [The Second Boarding](/scenarios/northern-lights/the-second-boarding/#spend-the-warning).
- A rail gun answer to a CSA breaching shuttle is exactly the kind of loud, committed, gloriously stupid the preface endorses. Let them.
- The mirror, inverted: the CSA out-equips the crew — *issued, one grade better* — except for Vantage, whose showroom kit out-equips the marines. The one line item on this crew the CSA's doctrine didn't price in is a tourist.

## Hooks (draft)

- He can leave whenever he wants. That's the thing nobody else on this crew can say, and everybody knows it — the question is what it takes to make him stop wanting to stay.
- Treats contracts like rounds of golf: keeps score, buys the drinks after, remembers every shot. Says "when this stops being fun" the way other people say "when I retire."
