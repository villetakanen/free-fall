## In this scenario

- **The suit question, on purpose:** the Chameleon Skin is not a rated EVA suit. Crossing to the Whisper means choosing — bind the Workhorse EVA suit *on top* (*+2 Body — pool drops to 3*) and lose the silhouette, or trust the skin's seals for the crossing and be the ghost the approach plan needs. This is the preface's "Binding heavy armor before an EVA is a commitment" lesson, handed to the player as a dilemma instead of a rule.
- In act two, the mirror inverts: the CSA runs the crew's own no-witnesses playbook, and Sable is the one crew member who can *be somewhere else* when it executes.
