# The Spectre

| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sable | CSA (ex-military) | 38 | F |

Every crew has someone who goes in first and is never seen doing it. Not the loudest gun — the quietest one. The Spectre is how a boarding starts before anyone knows it has: a body already inside, in the dark, deciding which way the fight will go before the fight is told it exists.

Sable learned it in a CSA uniform — regular service, ground and black work, never the space branch. She left with the tradecraft and none of the loyalty. She prefers equipment she can understand and keep working. She uses electronics, but doesn’t mistake them for something she can trust.

## Sheet

- **Class:** The Spectre (Skill: Infiltration)
- **Initial Attributes:** Body 12, Ghost 8, Mind 6
- **Action Pool:** 5d20
- **Harm slots:** 3, initially empty
- **Gear Loadout — reliable, traditional, no chrome:**
  - Void Marine: combat EVA suit, AV 2. *[Binds 3 Body + 1 Ghost]*
  - Heavy Kinetic Assault Rifle: DV 3, ballistic, no energy cells to run dry. *[Binds 4 Body]*
  - Combat knife — the backup that never jams. *[Binds 0 — light gear]*
- **Calculations:**
  - Body Binding: 3 (suit) + 4 (rifle) = 7 · Ghost Binding: 1 (suit) = 1
- **Current Attributes:**
  - **Body:** 12 − 7 = **5**
  - **Mind:** 6 − 0 = **6**
  - **Ghost:** 8 − 1 = **7** (barely touched — she spent nothing on stealth gear, because the stealth is the *class*)
- **Heavy option — Hardshell Combat Exoskeleton** (FRM 24 / SYS 6, AV 4). She keeps a frame racked for the jobs that are a stand-up fight, not a sneak — the reliable military tank, not the exotic stuff. In it the rifle mounts to a hardpoint (no Body binding), the frame soaks the hits, and she trades her ghost for a wall: *[Binds 3 Ghost]* → Ghost 8 − 3 = **5**, Body free. A Spectre in a Hardshell is a contradiction on paper and a very bad morning in a corridor.

## Starting position

At the [crew briefing](/scenarios/northern-lights/briefing/), choose where you first deploy:

- **Separate advance scout:** approach ahead of the main boarding group to observe defenders and find a usable route. If discovered, you have less immediate support.
- **Point operator with the main boarding group:** screen its chosen entry and protect the first teammates across. You move with the group instead of independently choosing your approach.

Infiltration works in either your Void Marine suit or the Hardshell. Their movement capability does not grant an unseen crossing or put you aboard automatically: establish the route, transport, and entry clearance for your chosen gear. Regroup if a separate approach fails. During a ram, anyone riding the vessel stays in crash protection until impact; a scout already outside keeps their actual position.

## In this scenario

- You learned boarding work in CSA service. You know their drills, their equipment, and what their orders leave unsaid.
- Infiltration is your training. You retain it in either the Void Marine or the Hardshell; your equipment and the available route still determine what is possible.

## Hooks (draft)

- Keeps a paper notebook. Actual paper. Says anything that can be hacked, will be — and she has watched it happen to operators she trusted.
- Left the CSA over an order she followed, not one she refused. Doesn't discuss it. The no-witnesses clause on this contract sat in her gut wrong from the first read, and she took the job anyway.
