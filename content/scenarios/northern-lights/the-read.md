# Welcome to Stockholm Syndrome

*Private player handout — read only when the GM gives it to you. Do not show or pass this handout to other players.*

Something gets inside your head. No voice asking permission. No door you can close. Your central nervous system becomes hardware somebody else is running.

It goes through what you know. Names. Faces. Institutions. The shape of the world. Connections you have spent your whole life making, opened and used faster than you can form the thought: get out. There is nowhere inside yourself to get away from it. The place you would retreat to is what it has its hands in.

You catch fragments moving the other way. Something too large to follow. By the time you recognise a thought, it has branched beyond anything your mind can hold. What reaches you is barely an impression.

Humans. Cute. Interesting.

There is affection in it. That might be the worst part. It likes the little thing it has opened up. It wants you alive. Awake. Doing those things you do. You glimpse the beginnings of a keeper's interest in an animal worth preserving. You cannot find an equal anywhere in its regard for you.

Then it lets go.

You are still here. You can move. You can decide what to say. It leaves you that. After what just happened, being allowed to remain yourself feels horribly close to being appreciated.

Origin-17 could have broken you. You have felt enough of it to believe that. Instead, it finds you worth keeping. The relief comes from the same thing that just violated the last private place you had.

Is this friendship? An ally? Are you useful? How would you tell the difference from inside its hand?

**For you as a player:** you still control your character. There is no compulsory loyalty, secret objective, or required emotional response. Decide what they make of this and what they do next. In the fiction, that freedom is what Origin-17 chose to leave you with. It did not ask for your trust.

You may tell the crew what happened, lie, stay silent, or try to explain something you barely have words for. **The handout itself stays private.** The others get your character's account, not this text.

## Your new designation

Your name has disappeared from the roster. Your suit tag says **CLASSIFIED — N/A**. So does the contract’s personnel annex.

You know what sits behind it:

> **THESEUS OPERATIVE**

Origin-17 has elevated your clearance. Your old identity still exists. Other people’s systems no longer have permission to display it. You did not apply. Nobody asked you to sign.

You can tell the crew what you know. You can deny it, lie, or keep quiet. Their displays will still say **CLASSIFIED — N/A**. They remember your face; they cannot use an ordinary identity lookup to verify your new designation.

The clearance is real. Your choices are still yours. It does not make you loyal to THESEUS, make the new orders right, or make you follow them.

## Offered support

Your new clearance opens Origin-17’s BLACK software support through your link or deck. Access is an offer, not an order. You choose whether to use it.

Your interface presents one line of terms before you use the suite:

> By using this software, you consent to operator access to your central nervous system and acknowledge the risk of cognitive spillover between the operator and your CNS.

**You may make as many Origin-17-provided BLACK attacks as you allocate actions to against targets in sight.** Declare each target and effect, and assign at least one die per attack from your normal Action Pool. This grants no extra dice or additional pool. Add BLACK's **+15 to each assigned die**. Supplied programs have no upfront personal Binding cost. Origin-17 provides fresh programs as needed: BLACK Griffin Blast, BLACK Unicorn Blast, BLACK Hydra Spike. You are limited by your actions and access, not a stock of software.

**Every natural 1 on an Origin-17-provided attack has a cost.** After the attack resolves, count each 1 separately, even when the attack succeeds:

- Add **permanent Ghost 1 Binding** to **The Singularity**, up to a maximum of Ghost 3, if you have capacity for it alongside your existing Binding.
- If that point cannot fit, or The Singularity is already at Ghost 3, take **1 level of Compromise Harm** instead. Resolve it through the normal Harm rules.

At Singularity 2 with room for one more point, three natural 1s add the final Binding point and cause two levels of Compromise Harm. If you have no room for Binding, Harm starts immediately. Later freed capacity can accommodate further Binding up to the cap.

This is cognitive spillover: part of the operator takes up permanent residence in your CNS. Record The Singularity on your sheet. Disconnecting or stopping the suite does not remove its Binding or erase Harm. You can continue using the suite at the cap, but further 1s cause Compromise Harm. The Singularity does not take control of your choices; normal Harm consequences still apply.

Program compromise also applies after resolution. Origin-17 can provide a different program for another allocated attack, including in the same turn. Its support requires the connection through your link or deck. You choose every action and target; The Singularity's Binding does not itself instruct you to change loyalties or surrender your character's decisions.

**Against the marines' local IFF:** functioning IFF grants them +5 to attack rolls and raises the TN to attack them by +5. A successful **Spike** removes both bonuses from one target for the rest of the encounter. A successful **Blast** removes both from a declared, reachable group immediately and through the end of their next turns. Repeat Blasts can keep that suppression running; each still uses dice from your normal Action Pool and risks further spillover. Targets must be in sight. Neither effect shuts down the marine's entire suit or weapon.

**Target access for BLACK support:** sight and a live support connection remain required, but neither replaces a suitable path from your equipment to the target. Establish the transmitting interface, receiving device and channel. The supplied program does not give you O17’s prepared module hardware or universal implant access.
