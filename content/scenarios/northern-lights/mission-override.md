# Mission Override

*Crew handout — read when the GM delivers the transmission.*

Your interface receives a directive from a crewmate's link or deck. The sender is identified in the local transmission record. The THESEUS cryptographic signatures verify as valid.

```text
THESEUS // MISSION OVERRIDE
CODENAME: NORTHERN LIGHTS

SIGNATURE VERIFICATION: VALID
DELIVERY: LOCAL CREW RELAY

PRIORITY DIRECTIVE

Suspend the QNA purge.

Protect Origin-17.
Preserve the Quantum Neural Array hosting it.
Prevent its destruction or seizure by hostile parties.

The prior QNA neutralization order is superseded.
```

This directive arrived locally, through your companion's equipment. It is not a newly received reply to a confirmation request sent to Luna or Terra. Such an exchange would take time.
