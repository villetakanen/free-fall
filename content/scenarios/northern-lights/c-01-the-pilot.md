# The Pilot

| Callsign | Origin | Age | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vector | Luna | 34 | F |

The assistant handles the long transit. Vector handles the approach: close maneuvering, boarding runs, and getting a damaged shuttle back to the ship.

On a mission, the pilot wears the "Tiger" — an elegant, hard-space-enhanced take on an MMA fighting kit. The suit makes her a walking close-quarters menace, from hand-to-hand tactics to ship-side corridor matches.

## Sheet

- **Class:** The Pilot (Skill: Vehicle Handling)
- **Initial Attributes:** Mind 12, Ghost 8, Body 6
- **Action Pool:** 5d20
- **Harm slots:** 3, initially empty
- **Gear Loadout:**
  - TGI-22 "Tiger" Close Combat EXO: FRM 6 / SYS 12, AV 2 — grants Close Combat Tactics while piloted; climbs on magnetic + gecko grapplers, no thrusters. *[Binds 3 Ghost + 1 Mind while piloted]*
    - Right hand, Assault hardpoint — Plasma Lance: DV 3, Piercing, recoilless twin-tube breaching gun. **Single Use** — grants Breaching for that one firing; mind the backblast. *[Hardpoint-mounted — no Pilot Binding]*
    - Left hand, Support hardpoint — Personal Defence Droid Array: autonomous tazer drones screening Close range, 1 Harm nonlethal. *[Hardpoint-mounted — no Pilot Binding]*
  - EVA Suit (Workhorse): AV 1 (Physical). *[Binds 2 Body]*
  - Micro-Missile Pistol: DV 2. *[Binds 1 Mind]*
  - Neural Link: data port — jack into the helm. *[Binds 3 Ghost]*
  - Arthropod Sensors: personal sensor array. *[Binds 1 Mind]*
- **Calculations:**
  - Body Binding: 2 (suit) = 2 · Mind Binding: 1 (pistol) + 1 (sensors) = 2, +1 (Tiger suite) while piloted · Ghost Binding: 3 (link) = 3, +3 (Tiger) while piloted
- **Current Attributes:**
  - **Body:** 6 − 2 = **4**
  - **Mind:** 12 − 2 = **10** (deep reserve for flying under fire), **9** in the Tiger
  - **Ghost:** 8 − 3 = **5** — and **2** in the Tiger (the frame demands almost everything she has left)

**While piloting the shuttle:** add Body 1/Mind 1. With the listed personal kit, your maximum Current Body/Mind/Ghost is **3/9/5** outside the Tiger, or **3/8/2** while also piloting the Tiger. Shuttle FRM 18/SYS 12 and Tiger FRM 6/SYS 12 remain separate; spend from the machine involved in the action. Releasing Binding does not refill spent points.

## Starting position

At the [crew briefing](/scenarios/northern-lights/briefing/), choose where you first deploy:

- **Crew-vessel exterior:** cover the others’ deployment while directing the shuttle through an optical or RF link. You retain a clear view of the vessel but must cross later to join the interior action.
- **With the boarding team, in the Tiger:** direct insertion while linked, then bring your breaching and close-combat capability to the entry. Hull geometry may break optical contact; RF depends on shielding and interference.

You need the link, not a seat at the helm. Give the assistant a continuing flight plan before contact becomes uncertain. A lost link prevents new remote commands; routine flight can continue. Shuttle pilot Binding is Body 1/Mind 1. Commands to the parent transport do not impose pilot Binding. The Tiger has no EVA thrusters, so arrange a line, shuttle, connected breach, or another viable lift. During a ram, pilot from crash protection and deploy afterward; that protected position need not be the helm.

## In this scenario

- You handle damaged craft and difficult approaches. When a maneuver needs a pilot, establish your control link and take over from the assistant.
- Your Tiger has grapplers but no thrusters. Keep an attachment or recovery plan when working outside. The plasma lance fires once; keep its backblast clear of your crew.

## Hooks (draft)

- Flew corporate extraction shuttles before something went on the record. The record is sealed. The habit of checking exits is not.
- Talks to the ship more politely than to people. Trusts it less than either.
