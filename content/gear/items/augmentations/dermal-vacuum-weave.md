*Vat-grown dermis lattice that seals the body against vacuum.*

A full-body graft: the skin is replaced, layer by layer, with a pressure-competent weave that closes reflexively against vacuum, flash-cold, and hostile atmosphere. It is not a suit — there is nothing to put on and nothing to tear off. Paired with an [Internal Oxygenator](/gear/augmentations/), it lets the owner survive hard vacuum in their own skin: minutes of working exposure, not hours, with eyes and ears paying the toll first.

**Effect:** Dermal Environmental Sealing — protects against vacuum, pressure loss, temperature extremes, and contact toxins without external gear. Full vacuum endurance (short, working durations) requires an independent air source such as an Internal Oxygenator.

**Binding rationale:** Body 2 — a second skin's constant metabolic upkeep, and the deep graft interface where the weave anchors to what's left of the original surface.
