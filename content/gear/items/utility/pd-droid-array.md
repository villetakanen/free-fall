*A shoal of palm-sized defence drones orbiting their principal, tazing anything that closes.*

Bodyguard hardware: the array holds a loose sphere around its owner, flags approaching threats, and intercepts on its own initiative — a drone attaches and discharges, dropping the target's nervous system out from under them. Nonlethal by design; the exec market frowns on collateral headlines.

**Effect:** Autonomously intercepts threats entering Close range; a connecting drone inflicts 1 level of nonlethal Harm (taze). The array is a screen, not a weapon — it buys seconds and distance. Susceptible to area effects, EW, and concentrated fire (Vulnerable Swarm), potentially triggering Limited Use.

**Binding rationale:** Ghost 2 — the array runs itself, but a cloud of armed drones keyed to your posture and threat instincts keeps a standing claim on composure.
