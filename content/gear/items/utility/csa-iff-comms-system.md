*Know who to shoot. Keep command informed.*

CSA identification, targeting and communications hardware, supplied as an external unit or an internal cybernetic installation. Implanting or removing the internal version requires surgery. Both versions bind Body 1 and Mind 1; mounting the external version on an exo’s Support hardpoint adds no pilot Binding. Normal Load Bearing applies. A suit cannot carry the cost of an implant; a Neural Link can offset its Mind cost when operating the system through that link.

**IFF:** While functioning, add +5 to each die on attack rolls and +5 to the TN of attacks against the user. This is targeting support, not armor. Switching off or disabling IFF removes both bonuses. Identification uses available records and the operator’s clearance.

**COMMS:** Analog laser-point voice links carry spoken orders. Separate data links relay selected sensor views and identification data. Establish connected endpoints and a usable channel; feeds convey what their sensors can observe. Classified records still require the recipient’s own clearance.

Target functions separately under [Hacking & Cyber Warfare](/core-rulebook/hacking-and-cyber-warfare/). Access to analog voice requires compromising a comm endpoint. Suppressing IFF leaves working communications available; destroying the whole package can remove both. Internal installation is an augmentation, with access dependent on a suitable channel reaching it.

**Binding rationale:** Hardware draws on the body; tracking identification and team feeds occupies attention.
