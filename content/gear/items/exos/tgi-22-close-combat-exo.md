*NCI-built exoskeleton for boarding melee and zero-G close quarters.*

Where the Hardshell absorbs punishment, the Tiger avoids it: a light frame wrapped around a very fast machine. It carries no thrusters — it *climbs*, magnetic and gecko grapplers on hands and feet taking it across hulls, bulkheads, and ceilings at a sprint, in any orientation, in vacuum or out. The high Systems score drives the Close Combat Suite, a reflex-prediction stack that reads posture, thrust, and blade-line faster than a human nervous system — and while piloted it grants the **Close Combat Tactics** skill outright. In the Tiger, close combat is something the machine knows and the pilot rides.

**Effect:** Grants Close Combat Tactics while piloted. High Systems for reflex-driven melee; hardpoint-mounted weapons add no Pilot Binding; Load Bearing is dedicated to its melee rack. Light Frame — it dodges, it does not tank. No thrusters: separated from a surface, it is cargo.

**Binding rationale:** Ghost 3 + Mind 1 — surrendering your reflexes to predator logic mid-fight demands composure most people don't have (the machine moves first and tells you afterwards), and the Close Combat Suite's overlay keeps a running claim on the pilot's attention.
