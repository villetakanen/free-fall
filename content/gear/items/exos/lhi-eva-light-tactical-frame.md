*A stabilized mount, precise thrusters, and very little between you and incoming fire.*

Luna Heavy Industries builds bespoke high-end hardware around direct neural control. The L-hit carries a weapon and a support system through open space. Magnetic anchors secure a perch; thrusters move it. Firing stabilizers permit mounted fire in free flight without adding an attack bonus.

**Interface:** Requires an installed Neural Link. The frame’s Mind 4 pilot Binding uses half the Link’s shared Mind 8 software/systems allowance, leaving 4 for active programs. The implant still binds Ghost 3. Mounted equipment adds no pilot Binding.

**Effect:** FRM 3, SYS 10 and AV 0 make position essential. The mission computer runs software through the Link; establish a suitable transmitting interface and target channel for every intrusion. The frame’s sensors identify targets without automatically providing access to their electronics.

**Binding rationale:** Mind 4 — directing thrust, stabilization, anchors and mounted systems requires sustained concentration.
