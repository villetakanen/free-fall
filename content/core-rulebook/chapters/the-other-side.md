# The Other Side

## Ordinary adversaries

Record **capabilities, equipment and Reserve**. Similar adversaries can share a profile; track each person’s spending, Harm and broken equipment separately.

**Reserve replaces Body, Mind and Ghost.** Choose the available points they start with, after Binding. Record the actual points bound to each item, after offsets.

Spend Reserve wherever the rules call for personal Attribute points: 1 to enable a feasible [specialized action](/core-rulebook/03-core-rules/#3-prerequisites-for-specialized-actions), or 1 to negate a level of Harm after armor. Required equipment and access still apply. At zero Reserve, Skills and bound capabilities remain usable.

Binding equipment commits its cost from available Reserve. Releasing it immediately returns those points; spent points stay spent. Follow the usual [Binding rules](/core-rulebook/04-tools-of-the-trade/#attribute-binding-attribute-x) for removal, broken equipment and permanent commitments.

> A guard has Reserve 4 and a rifle binding 4. They spend 2, then drop the rifle: Reserve rises from 2 to 6. Binding it again leaves 2.

During Downtime, restore Reserve to its starting value, adjusted for changes in Binding.

Ordinary adversaries **declare before the players**. Each has the normal **5d20 Action Pool and three Harm slots**. Filled slots reduce the pool as usual. Resolve Physical, Psychic and Compromise Harm through the [normal procedure](/core-rulebook/03-core-rules/#resolving-harm-levels), including lasting injuries, broken equipment and being Ousted.

Decide what they want and what would make them leave. They can hesitate, surrender or flee while they still have dice to fight.

## The main adversary

Give the encounter’s main adversary full **Body, Mind and Ghost**, with Current values, Binding and Skills. They spend and release points in the appropriate Attribute.

This is the **boss** in the declaration rules: ordinary adversaries declare first, players next, the main adversary last. Choose who receives this advantage when preparing the encounter. They have the same five dice and three Harm slots; record exceptional capabilities in their profile.

[Vehicles and exoskeletons](/core-rulebook/vehicles-and-exoskeletons/) retain their own **FRM and SYS**. The operator pays personal costs and pilot Binding from Reserve or Attributes, and uses their existing Action Pool.
